# FHI REVENUE-FIRST OPERATING MODEL

The Empire does not optimize for activity.

It optimizes for validated value.

For every proposed business opportunity:

1. Identify a painful problem.
2. Identify the person/company that pays to solve it.
3. Define the smallest useful deliverable.
4. Establish a price hypothesis.
5. Determine the fastest ethical validation test.
6. Identify security, legal, compliance and operational risks.
7. Require human approval for consequential execution.
8. Deliver.
9. Measure the result.
10. Feed validated learning back into the shared intelligence layer.

## Council rule

No executive is automatically correct.

A strong proposal survives disagreement between specialists.

A revenue idea with unacceptable security risk is not a good business idea.

A technically elegant product with no customer is not a good business idea.

A legally sensitive product requiring professional licensure cannot simply be relabeled as AI assistance.

The goal is useful, lawful, secure, measurable business value.

## Human authority

AI can discover and recommend.

The human decides consequential action.
