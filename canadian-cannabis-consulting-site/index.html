About Your Consultant

Derek Francisco

Derek Francisco has spent over two decades at the intersection of cannabis advocacy, constitutional law strategy, and business development in Canada. He is the founder of Francisco Holdings Inc. and the architect of Canada's most comprehensive cannabis legal education platform — CCLDR.net.

Derek has guided over 100 cannabis businesses through the Health Canada licensing process — from micro-cultivation startups to multi-site licensed producers. His consulting clients benefit from hard-won regulatory insight, direct Health Canada experience, and a legal framework grounded in the Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms.

He is an educator, advocate, and consultant — not a lawyer. Every engagement is built on honesty, documented strategy, and the relentless pursuit of zero license denials.
