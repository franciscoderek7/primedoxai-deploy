EXPANDED DISCLOSURE REQUEST — VIDEO & WARRANT PACKAGE
CCLDR.NET — Educational Template — NOT Legal Advice

This is the "Warrior+" template referenced on documents.html (doc-tag:
Warrant Review / ITO Request / Warrior+). Extends the free "Disclosure
Request Letter — Standard" already wired into downloadTemplate() with
specific demands targeting surveillance video, warrant materials, and
informant information.

---

[DATE]

To: Crown Attorney, [COURTHOUSE NAME]
Re: R v [YOUR NAME], Court File No. [FILE NUMBER]
Next Court Date: [DATE]

Dear Crown Counsel,

I am the accused in the above-noted matter, appearing [self-represented /
through counsel]. Further to my earlier disclosure request dated [DATE OF
PRIOR REQUEST, if any], and pursuant to R v Stinchcombe, [1991] 3 SCR 326
and its progeny, I request the following SPECIFIC materials, which I
understand may not have been captured by a general disclosure request:

SURVEILLANCE / VIDEO MATERIALS
1. All body-worn camera footage from every officer present at the scene,
   in unredacted/original format, including footage before and after the
   period directly relevant to my arrest;
2. All dashboard/in-car camera footage from all patrol vehicles present;
3. Any third-party surveillance footage obtained by police (e.g.,
   business security cameras, residential doorbell cameras) relating to
   this matter, and the date police obtained it;
4. Any audio recordings of police radio communications/dispatch logs for
   this incident.

WARRANT MATERIALS (if a warrant was relied upon)
5. A complete copy of the search warrant and the supporting Information
   to Obtain (ITO), including all attachments and any sealed portions —
   if any portion is sealed, I request notice of the sealing order and the
   process for challenging it;
6. All drafts or prior versions of the ITO, if they exist and are not
   privileged;
7. The name and badge number of the affiant who swore the ITO, and the
   name of the issuing justice.

INFORMANT / TIP INFORMATION
8. To the extent it does not engage informer privilege, the general
   nature of any tip or source information that led to this
   investigation, sufficient for me to assess the reliability and
   currency of the information relied upon (I am not asking for the
   informant's identity, which I understand may be privileged);
9. Any internal police notes assessing the credibility or reliability of
   the source.

OFFICER AND PROCEDURAL RECORDS
10. The complete handwritten and typed notes of every officer involved,
    not just the officer who prepared the occurrence report;
11. Any disciplinary record relevant to truthfulness or use-of-force
    findings for the officers involved, to the extent disclosable under
    current case law on third-party records and officer discipline
    history;
12. Any internal use-of-force report, if force was used during the
    arrest.

The Crown's disclosure obligation is continuous, and I request that any of
the above materials that come into existence or into the Crown's
possession after this letter be produced immediately. Please confirm
receipt and provide a date by which I can expect production, or advise
specifically which items are being withheld and on what basis (e.g.,
privilege, irrelevance) so that the issue can be addressed before the
court if necessary.

Respectfully,
[YOUR NAME]
[YOUR CONTACT INFORMATION]
[DATE]

Have this reviewed by qualified legal counsel before use. Some items above
(e.g., officer discipline records, informant information) involve
third-party records and privilege rules that are fact- and
jurisdiction-specific — what is actually disclosable depends on case law
current at the time of your request, which counsel should confirm.
