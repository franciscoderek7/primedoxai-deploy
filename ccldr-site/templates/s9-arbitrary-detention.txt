NOTICE OF APPLICATION
(Charter of Rights and Freedoms, s.9 and s.24(2))

TAKE NOTICE that the Accused, [YOUR FULL LEGAL NAME], will make an
application to this Honourable Court on [DATE], at [TIME], or as soon
after as the application can be heard, at [COURTHOUSE NAME AND ADDRESS].

THE APPLICATION IS FOR:

1. A declaration that the detention of the Accused on [DATE] at
   [LOCATION] was arbitrary, contrary to section 9 of the Canadian
   Charter of Rights and Freedoms;

2. An order excluding [DESCRIBE THE EVIDENCE flowing from the detention —
   e.g., statements made, items found during a subsequent search] pursuant
   to section 24(2) of the Charter;

3. Such further and other relief as this Honourable Court deems just.

THE GROUNDS FOR THE APPLICATION ARE:

1. On [DATE], the Accused was stopped/detained by [OFFICER NAME(S)/BADGE
   NUMBER(S)] of the [POLICE SERVICE] at or near [LOCATION], for
   [DESCRIBE THE STATED REASON, if any was given];

2. Under R v Mann, 2004 SCC 52, police may briefly detain an individual for
   investigative purposes only where they have reasonable grounds to
   suspect, based on objectively discernible facts, that the individual is
   connected to a particular crime and that detention is reasonably
   necessary;

3. [Explain why the detention does not meet the Mann standard — e.g., the
   officer could not articulate specific facts connecting the Accused to
   any offence; the stop was based on a generalized suspicion, a hunch, or
   profiling rather than objectively discernible facts];

4. The detention exceeded the scope and duration reasonably necessary for
   the stated investigative purpose — see Mann at paras 34-35 on the
   limited scope of a lawful investigative detention (it does not, on its
   own, authorize a search beyond a protective pat-down for safety);

5. [If applicable: the Accused was not informed of the reason for the
   detention, compounding the s.9 breach and engaging s.10(a)];

6. Applying the R v Grant, 2009 SCC 32 framework — (a) seriousness of the
   police conduct, (b) impact on the Accused's Charter interests, and
   (c) society's interest in a trial on the merits — exclusion of the
   resulting evidence is warranted. [Apply the three factors to your facts.]

THIS APPLICATION will be supported by an affidavit of the Accused (and any
other supporting affidavits) to be served and filed prior to the hearing
date, and on such further evidence as counsel may advise.
