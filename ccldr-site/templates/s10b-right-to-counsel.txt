NOTICE OF APPLICATION
(Charter of Rights and Freedoms, s.10(b) and s.24(2))

TAKE NOTICE that the Accused, [YOUR FULL LEGAL NAME], will make an
application to this Honourable Court on [DATE], at [TIME], or as soon
after as the application can be heard, at [COURTHOUSE NAME AND ADDRESS].

THE APPLICATION IS FOR:

1. A declaration that the Accused's right to retain and instruct counsel
   without delay, guaranteed by section 10(b) of the Canadian Charter of
   Rights and Freedoms, was violated following [arrest/detention] on
   [DATE] at [LOCATION];

2. An order excluding the statement(s) made by the Accused on [DATE] at
   [TIME/LOCATION] pursuant to section 24(2) of the Charter;

3. Such further and other relief as this Honourable Court deems just.

THE GROUNDS FOR THE APPLICATION ARE:

1. On [DATE], the Accused was arrested/detained by [OFFICER NAME(S)/BADGE
   NUMBER(S)] of the [POLICE SERVICE] at or near [LOCATION];

2. Section 10(b) imposes both an informational duty (to advise the
   detainee of the right to counsel) and an implementational duty (to
   provide a reasonable opportunity to exercise that right, and to hold
   off questioning until that opportunity has been provided, absent
   urgent/exigent circumstances or a valid waiver);

3. [DESCRIBE THE BREACH — choose what applies:]
   (a) The Accused was not advised of the right to counsel at all, or not
       advised promptly upon arrest/detention; OR
   (b) The Accused requested to speak with a lawyer, but was not given a
       reasonable opportunity to do so (e.g., no access to a phone, no
       privacy, no list of duty counsel, excessive delay) before being
       questioned; OR
   (c) Police continued to question the Accused, or elicited a statement,
       after the request for counsel was made and before the opportunity
       to consult was provided;

4. The statement(s) at issue were obtained as a direct result of this
   breach and are causally connected to it;

5. Applying the R v Grant, 2009 SCC 32 framework — (a) seriousness of the
   Charter-infringing conduct, (b) impact on the Accused's Charter-
   protected interests (the right against self-incrimination and to
   counsel is foundational), and (c) society's interest in an adjudication
   on the merits — exclusion of the statement(s) is warranted under
   s.24(2). [Apply the three factors to your specific facts.]

THIS APPLICATION will be supported by an affidavit of the Accused (and any
other supporting affidavits) to be served and filed prior to the hearing
date, and on such further evidence as counsel may advise.
