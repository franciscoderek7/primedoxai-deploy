The Respondents

Who's Being Held Accountable

Demand letters have been sent to each respondent setting out the factual basis of the claim and the relief sought. This case is at the pre-litigation demand stage — no Statement of Claim has been filed yet.

CIBC
Canadian Imperial Bank of Commerce — named respondent in connection with fraud and theft allegations arising from account-handling conduct affecting Derek Francisco. Five documented call abandonments; supervisors Andrew Slopianka (#5420) and Yaromir (#2000298) hung up rather than resolve the dispute.
Kudos
Named respondent in connection with the same pattern of conduct underlying the fraud and theft claims, and related disability discrimination allegations. Specific conduct: unauthorized hookup fees, an $823.21 unauthorized account debit, and a stopped-payment check disputed as fraud.
Telus
Named respondent in connection with disability discrimination and related conduct contributing to the financial harm at issue in this claim.
