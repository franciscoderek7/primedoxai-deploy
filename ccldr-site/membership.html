SCC Authority Preview

Free teaser — four landmark cases that shape every argument Doc Weedlaw makes in court.

R v Grant
2009 SCC 32

Established the modern framework for excluding evidence obtained in breach of the Charter under s.24(2). The foundation of every exclusion argument.

🔒 Full analysis — Library tier+
R v Collins
1987 1 SCR 265

Pre-Grant standard for s.24(2) exclusion. Introduced "fair trial" and "administration of justice" considerations. Still cited in legacy arguments.

🔒 Full analysis — Library tier+
PHS Community v Canada
2011 SCC 44

Federal Minister's refusal to grant exemption for Insite held to violate s.7. Established that arbitrary denial of life-saving health services breaches Charter rights.

🔒 Full analysis — Library tier+
Uber Technologies v Heller
2020 SCC 16

Arbitration clause held unconscionable where it imposed prohibitive cost on vulnerable party. Key precedent for fighting unfair standard form contracts — including telecom agreements.

🔒 Full analysis — Library tier+
