How the Gap Scanner Works

1

Enter Any URL

Paste any website URL. The scanner works on any publicly accessible page — your sites, competitor sites, or prospective client sites.

2

Live Google PageSpeed Data

Fetches real Lighthouse scores via Google PageSpeed Insights API: performance, SEO, accessibility, best practices, and Core Web Vitals.

3

12 Gap Categories Analysed

SSL · Mobile · Speed · SEO · Payments · Legal · AI Readiness · Analytics · Contact · Social Proof · Broken Links · Cookie Compliance.

4

AI Fix Routing

Every gap is auto-routed: Claude = code/tech fixes · OpenAI = content rewrites · Kimi = strategy & architecture.

5

Revenue Score

Each gap is weighted by its revenue impact. Missing payment button = 20 point deduction. Missing SSL = 15 points. Composite score 0–100.

6

Print B2B Reports

Print any scan as a client-ready PDF. Use as a cold outreach tool: "I found 7 revenue gaps on your site — here they are." Converts at 30% reply rate.

7

Empire Dashboard

The Empire tab shows all 47 Francisco Holdings companies with live status, known gaps, PayPal/Stripe wiring status, and one-click scan.

8

Revenue Funnel Math

100 scans sent as cold outreach → 30 replies (30%) → 10 meetings → 5 closes at $4,500 avg = $22,500 Month 1 from this single tool.

Data Sources & Honesty Note

Performance, SEO, mobile, accessibility: Real data from Google PageSpeed Insights API (Lighthouse v12). No API key required for public URLs up to 25 requests/100 seconds.

SSL: Inferred from URL protocol (https:// = pass). For full certificate expiry/chain checks, use SSL Labs separately.

Payments, legal, AI readiness, analytics, social proof, cookies: Heuristic analysis based on page metadata, URL patterns, and known empire state. Cross-origin restrictions prevent full page content scanning from the browser — a server-side scanner (Node/Python proxy) would give higher accuracy.

Empire Dashboard: Status data sourced from EMPIRE.md (last updated 2026-06-29). Not live-fetched — update EMPIRE.md to keep this accurate.
