## FAQ

**Can you draft an affidavit?**
PrimeDox offers AI-assisted document generation, including legal templates that can help you prepare drafts such as affidavits. Generated documents are templates and starting points — for anything filed with a court, have a qualified professional review the final document before submission.

**How do I track my case?**
PrimeDox includes case tracking tools so you can follow the status and key dates of your matter in one place. For specific account or case access issues, your request will be routed internally to the correct specialist.
