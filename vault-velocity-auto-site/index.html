⬡ The Process

How It Works

From Dubai showroom to your garage in 4 steps.

1
Browse & Select
Choose from our Dubai-sourced inventory or request a specific car. Derek sources directly from UAE dealerships and private sellers.
2
Reserve & Pay
Secure your vehicle with a deposit via wire transfer, Bitcoin, or PayPal. Full payment before shipping. All transactions documented.
3
Dubai to Port
Your car is inspected, documented, and shipped from Dubai (Jebel Ali Port). Full customs paperwork included. RoRo or container shipping.
4
Delivered to You
Your vehicle arrives at your nearest port, cleared through customs. Local delivery available in Canada and the USA.
