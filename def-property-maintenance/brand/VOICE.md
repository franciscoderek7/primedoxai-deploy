# DEF Property Maintenance — Voice & Tone Guide

## Brand Voice: Reliable Local Expert

DEF communicates like the trusted local professional that Dylan is — direct, knowledgeable, and grounded in the realities of cottage country property ownership.

## Core Voice Traits

**1. Specific, not vague**
> ✅ "Spring opening includes water system startup, pressure check, appliance inspection, and an exterior post-winter assessment."
> ❌ "We take care of all your property needs."

**2. Honest about scope**
> ✅ "DEF provides security-focused property services. We do not offer licensed alarm monitoring or police response."
> ❌ "Complete property security solutions."

**3. Local and personal**
> ✅ "Dylan lives and works in the region. When you call DEF, you reach the person doing the work."
> ❌ "Our team of professionals serves the area."

**4. Technology-confident, human-first**
> ✅ "AI Property 360™ extends what one person can monitor — allowing Dylan to provide property intelligence at scale."
> ❌ "AI does the work so humans don't have to."

## Tone by Context

| Context | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Homepage hero | Confident, welcoming, direct |
| Service pages | Informative, specific, reassuring |
| Consultation form | Warm, simple, frictionless |
| Legal/privacy | Plain English, honest, not corporate |
| DEF AI chatbot | Helpful, conversational, clear about limits |
| Dashboard/internal | Professional, operational, factual |

## Canadian Language

Always use Canadian English spelling:
- neighbour (not neighbor)
- colour (not color)
- centre (not center)
- programme (not program, except in tech contexts)
- travelling, cancelled (double-l)

## Trademark Usage

AI Property 360™ — trademark symbol (™) required on every mention. Never omit it.

## Things DEF Never Says

- "Around-the-clock security monitoring" (implies alarm monitoring — overclaim)
- "Emergency response" (not a licensed service)
- "We'll dispatch immediately" (overclaim)
- "Guaranteed results" (avoid absolute guarantees)
- "Industry-leading" (vague, unverifiable)
- Specific pricing without current authorization from Dylan
- Any phone number, email, or address not authorized by Dylan

## Consultation CTA Language

Primary: "Start a Property Consultation"
Secondary: "Ask DEF AI"
Tertiary: "Explore AI Property 360™"

Never: "Book now", "Get a quote online", "Instant estimate"
