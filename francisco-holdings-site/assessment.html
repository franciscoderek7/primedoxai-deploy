The problems it addresses

  • Staff hours lost to re-typing the same information between systems.

  • No measurement of where enquiries come from, so marketing spend cannot be judged.

What you receive

What this is not

It is not a penetration test, a security certification, a compliance audit or a performance audit. It makes no prediction about your revenue. Findings reflect what was observable on the date of the review.
