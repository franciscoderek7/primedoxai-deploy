Activation Guide

1

Download & Install

Download the installer for your platform above. On Android/iOS, tap Install in the store. On Windows/macOS, run the installer and follow the on-screen prompts. No admin rights required for basic protection.

2

Create or Sign In to Your Account

Open the app and enter your CyberGuard account email. If you purchased a plan at omniaguard.com/pricing, your account is already active — use the same email address.

3

Enter Your Activation Code

Your 16-character activation code was emailed after purchase. Enter it in the Activate License screen. Format: CGPA-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX. Check your spam folder if not received within 5 minutes.

4

Run Your First Scan

Tap Full Scan from the dashboard. The initial scan takes 2–8 minutes depending on device storage. Subsequent scans run in the background automatically and won't impact performance.

5

Enable Real-Time Protection

On Android, grant the Accessibility permission when prompted — this enables link-intercept protection. On iOS, enable the VPN & Device Management profile in Settings. On Windows/macOS, approve the system extension during first launch.

6

Need Help?

Contact support: omniaguard1@gmail.com. Include your activation code and platform. Response within 24 hours.
